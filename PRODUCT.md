# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clarity Share serves presenters who need to show detailed text, design work, or motion at substantially higher quality than a typical chat-platform screen share, and up to ten invited viewers who need a clear, installation-free browser experience. Presenters operate a live room: they choose a source, choose open or approval-required admission, monitor each independent connection, and resolve quality issues. Viewers arrive from a secure link, join directly by default, and focus on the shared content.

## Product Purpose

Clarity Share provides secure, expiring peer-to-peer screen sharing from one browser to as many as ten viewers, with public-link admission by default and optional presenter approval. It succeeds when starting a room is fast, access policy is unmistakable, high-detail content remains legible, connection quality is transparent, and recovery controls are available without exposing the underlying signaling complexity.

## Brand Personality

Focused, precise, and confident. The product should feel calm under live-session pressure and communicate technical state with the disciplined density and restraint associated with Linear.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketing dashboards, glass-heavy surfaces, oversized hero treatments inside the product, endless identical cards, novelty controls, gratuitous gradients, decorative motion, and playful copy that undermines trust during a live session.

## Design Principles

1. Put the live content first; controls and diagnostics should support the stream rather than compete with it.
2. Make state legible; admission policy, approval, connection, relay, capture, and recovery states must never be ambiguous.
3. Reveal depth progressively; keep the primary path compact while making advanced quality and diagnostic controls easy to reach.
4. Prefer earned familiarity; standard controls, predictable layout, and concise language reduce mistakes during a live share.
5. Treat privacy as visible product behavior; explain invitation security, peer-to-peer topology, and upload impact where decisions occur.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic HTML, complete keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, non-color state cues, appropriately announced live status, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable at narrow widths and high zoom.
